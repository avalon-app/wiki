# Avalon Wiki

*The Resistance: Avalon* is a hidden-role game. Players are either Loyal Servants of Arthur (representing good and honour) or Minions of Mordred (the evil side).

- **Good** wins by passing three quests.
- **Evil** wins by failing three quests; alternatively by assassinating Merlin at the end, or by preventing quests from ever being executed.

At the table, players may say anything at any time. **Discussion, deception, accusations, and deduction** are the levers by which one side outmanoeuvres the other.
