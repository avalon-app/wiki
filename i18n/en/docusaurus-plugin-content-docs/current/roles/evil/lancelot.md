# Lancelot · Evil

<RoleCard id="lancelot_evil" locale="en" />

**Faction**: Evil (Red, initial)

**Power**:

- At game start, the evil Lancelot is **seen by Merlin and the other evil players**, but **does not see** them.
- Under **Rule 1 / Rule 2**, alignment may swap during play — the two Lancelots trade sides; their victory is determined by their **end-of-game** alignment.
- Under **Rule 2**, while on the evil side, the Lancelot can **only play Fail** on quests.
- Under **Rule 3**, no swaps happen, but the two Lancelots can see each other.

See [Lancelot expansion](../../rules/expansions/lancelot.md) for swap mechanics.

> Lancelot alignment swaps happen silently between players; the role panel does not display the change, though vote restrictions still apply.
