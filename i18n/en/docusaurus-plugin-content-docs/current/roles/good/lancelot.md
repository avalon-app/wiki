# Lancelot · Good

<RoleCard id="lancelot_good" locale="en" />

**Faction**: Good (Blue, initial)

**Power**: Plays alongside the evil Lancelot.

- Under **Rule 1 / Rule 2**, alignments may swap during play — the two Lancelots trade sides.
- Under **Rule 3**, no swaps happen, but the two Lancelots can see each other.

See [Lancelot expansion](../../rules/expansions/lancelot.md) for swap mechanics.
