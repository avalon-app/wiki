# Roles Overview

Roles split into two factions. Entries marked `*` only appear with their respective expansion.

## Good (Blue)

| Role | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| [Merlin](./good/merlin.md) | Sees all evil players except Mordred; appears alongside Morgana to Percival |
| [Percival](./good/percival.md) | Sees Merlin and Morgana but cannot tell them apart |
| [Loyal Servant](./good/loyal-servant.md) | No special information; relies on discussion to find evil and protect Merlin |
| [Lancelot (Good)](./good/lancelot.md)* | Plays alongside the evil Lancelot; see the Lancelot expansion |

## Evil (Red)

| Role | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| [Assassin](./evil/assassin.md) | After 3 quest successes, may name Merlin to flip the win |
| [Morgana](./evil/morgana.md) | Appears as Merlin to Percival |
| [Mordred](./evil/mordred.md) | Hidden from Merlin |
| [Oberon](./evil/oberon.md) | Mutually hidden with other evil players |
| [Minion](./evil/minion.md) | Vanilla evil role |
| [Lancelot (Evil)](./evil/lancelot.md)* | Does not see fellow evil players, but is seen by them and by Merlin |

Full role metadata is maintained in [`data/roles.yaml`](https://github.com/avalon-app/wiki/blob/main/data/roles.yaml) and exposed at `@avalon-app/wiki/data/roles` after build.
