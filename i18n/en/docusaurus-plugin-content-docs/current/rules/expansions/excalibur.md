# Excalibur

## Rules

1. When proposing a team, the leader grants Excalibur to a **non-self** teammate.
2. If the team is approved, after the team has played their Success/Fail cards but **before results are revealed**, the Excalibur holder may choose to use it.
3. If used, the holder names a **non-self** team member; that player's quest card is **flipped**:
   - Success ↔ Fail
4. After the flip, the holder secretly **sees the original card** the target had played.
5. Excalibur has **no usage cap** across the game.

> A sword icon on a player's avatar marks the current Excalibur holder.
