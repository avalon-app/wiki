# Win Conditions

## Good (Blue) wins when

1. Three quests have succeeded, **and**
2. The Assassin **fails** to identify Merlin in the assassination phase.

## Evil (Red) wins when (any of)

1. Three quests have failed.
2. Five consecutive team proposals are rejected within a single quest.
3. After three quest successes, the Assassin **correctly** identifies Merlin in the assassination phase.

## On Assassination

- Usually opened by Evil after the third quest success, but the Assassin may strike **at any point in the game**.
- Only the Assassin chooses the target.
- Once executed, the result is final and the game ends.
